Scam reports

Students report listings they think are fake or unsafe. A listing reported by three different phone numbers within 30 days is hidden from students until you decide.

Call the landlord and, where possible, a reporter before deciding. Dismiss if the reports are unfounded (the listing shows again). Take down if it looks like a scam: the listing is unpublished, loses its verified badge and must be re-approved.
